# Teacher's Day Marketing Playbook

Use for China Teacher's Day planning. Treat it as a respect-and-relationship moment, not a generic gifting festival. First identify whether the brand serves teachers, students, parents, education, office workers, or the wider public; the relevant relationship, product role, and risk are different.

## Decision guardrails

- Do not pressure parents, students, or schools to buy gifts for teachers.
- Do not imply that a gift earns preferential treatment or use unverified teacher/student outcomes as proof.
- Do not use a teacher, school, student, or user story for commercial endorsement without required consent and platform-policy review.
- When education products/services are involved, verify the current platform policy before creator content or performance claims.
- A “thank-you” narrative must lead to a credible product/service experience, contribution, or useful action; a logo on a greeting card is not enough.

## Three distinct creative territories

### 1. 把被看见还给老师 / Recognition

**Big Idea:** 让每一份具体的影响被学生、家长或同行认真说出来。

**Participation:** Users submit a short “老师教会我的一件事” voice note, object, photo, or story; moderation selects stories with consent. Brand turns selected entries into a public thank-you wall, audio postcard, or community exhibition.

**Product entry:** Make the product a practical tool that reduces a teacher's real burden, improves a lesson/workday, or funds a verified classroom/community need. Do not claim educational outcomes without evidence.

**Spread:** One specific memory is easier to share than a generic blessing; prompt replies with “我也有一位这样的老师”. Brand account publishes creator/teacher/parent perspectives with a consistent story format.

**Risk/fallback:** Consent, privacy, and sentimental sameness. If user stories are insufficient, switch to a curated “100 个教会我的瞬间” editorial series using commissioned, cleared stories.

### 2. 老师的一节课 / Utility and experience

**Big Idea:** 让公众体验老师每天解决的真实小问题。

**Participation:** A 60-second classroom/workday challenge, decision simulator, or offline micro-workshop lets users complete a real task with constraints. Users unlock a practical benefit, donation, or product trial by finishing—not by purchasing a gift.

**Product entry:** Demonstrate a specific feature/service in the challenge, such as organization, communication, ergonomic support, or content creation. Define the proof point before production.

**Spread:** Challenge results, solution templates, and “我第一次理解了______” reaction clips. Creator roles: teacher/professional verifies the task; lifestyle creator interprets it; brand account hosts the tool and answers questions.

**Risk/fallback:** Avoid caricaturing teaching or gamifying hardship. If the interactive build is infeasible, use a creator-led “一节课拆解” content series with a downloadable tool/template.

### 3. 谢谢的下一步 / Community contribution

**Big Idea:** 把一句谢谢变成可追踪的支持行动。

**Participation:** Users nominate a school/community need from a vetted list, vote on resource allocation, volunteer time, or contribute an existing skill. The brand publishes progress milestones and completion evidence.

**Product entry:** The brand contributes product, service time, infrastructure, or matched resources that directly solve the selected need. Keep contribution claims specific and verifiable.

**Spread:** “我投给了______，因为______” nomination stories, monthly progress updates, partner/community co-publication, and a completion recap rather than one-day sentiment.

**Risk/fallback:** Charity-washing and opaque allocation. Partner with a qualified organization, set public eligibility and reporting rules, or pivot to an internal employee-volunteer program if external governance is not ready.

## Example phase plan

| Phase | User action | Brand action | Channel/asset | KPI |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 预热（T-21 to T-8） | Identify a story, task, or need | Publish call for participation and creator seeding | Brand account, creator shortlist, landing/collection page | Qualified submissions, completion rate |
| 引爆（T-7 to T+1） | Participate and share | Release flagship story/challenge; select high-quality entries for amplification | Hero video, topic page, PR, paid test | Qualified engagement, earned sharing |
| 持续（T+2 to T+21） | Return, vote, use tool, or volunteer | Publish progress and useful content; retarget engaged audiences to the relevant destination | CRM/private domain, search, remarketing | Return rate, intent action |
| 沉淀 | Subscribe, save, recommend | Archive tools, stories, and verified impact | Resource hub, newsletter/CRM | Save rate, opt-in, completion evidence |

## Current-trend use

Do not attach a meme by default. If the brief asks for a current meme, run `workflows/trend-scan.md`; a meme may decorate the entry point but must not replace the recognition, utility, or contribution mechanism above.
