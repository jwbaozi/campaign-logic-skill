# Node and Product-launch Playbook

Use after extracting every node, season, event and product-launch/important communication requirement from the user's material. This is a mechanism library, not a promise that every idea fits every brand. State missing product, audience, channel, budget, or rights inputs as `【需要补充】`.

## New product launch

Use when the core task is proving a product's new value, feature, usage scene, or reason to switch.

### Product Proof Lab

**Mechanism:** Turn the product's strongest claim into a transparent 60-second test, comparison, try-on, simulator, or challenge. Show the proof condition, not only the conclusion.

**Handoff:** Creator/brand content demonstrates the proof; a landing page, store appointment, mini-program, sample request, or live demo is the next step; engaged non-converters receive a different proof asset through remarketing.

**Risk:** Avoid unsubstantiated efficacy/comparative claims. Define evidence, disclaimer, and measurement before producing content.

### First-user Relay

**Mechanism:** Recruit a controlled first-user group to complete a specific task and pass a useful result/template/benefit to the next user, creating user-generated proof rather than a generic unboxing.

**Handoff:** Application → trial/onboarding → verified usage task → story/assessment → referral or purchase destination.

**Risk:** Do not use selected testimonials as universal proof; disclose sampling and obtain consent.

## Spring Festival / Chinese New Year

Use for a family, reunion, renewal, travel, local culture, or seasonal service-recovery moment. If the campaign is many months away, write `在实际发布前 T-14 至 T-7 重新扫描并做平台、权益与品牌安全核验`; do not use an early-year meme as future creative.

### New-year Useful Ritual

**Mechanism:** Let users create a practical new-year ritual, checklist, tool, service reservation, or shared family task that naturally requires the product.

**Handoff:** Content inspiration → personalized tool/template → store/e-commerce/service booking → post-holiday return/CRM reminder.

### Homecoming Skill Exchange

**Mechanism:** Users exchange one skill or helpful action across generations/communities; the brand offers a product-enabled solution or matched service rather than a generic red-envelope draw.

**Risk:** Avoid stereotypes, family pressure, cultural appropriation, and claims that the brand owns a traditional practice.

## Christmas

Use for year-end appreciation, small-group social rituals, winter comfort, wish-making, or post-year reflection. Adapt the cultural role to the market and audience; do not assume every audience celebrates it.

### Secret Kindness Relay

**Mechanism:** Users send an anonymous, specific act of help or appreciation; recipients unlock a small useful product/service benefit and can continue the relay.

**Handoff:** Entry content → gift/benefit configuration → recipient landing/commerce page → opt-in relationship/return visit.

### Year-end Small Wins

**Mechanism:** Ask users to make a 30-second “small win” recap; the product enters as a specific enabler of rest, connection, creativity, or progress—not as a forced holiday sticker.

**Risk:** Be inclusive of non-celebrants and do not trivialize hardship or religious meaning.

## How to use current trends across nodes

For each mechanism, scan near the publishing date for an approved current format, sound, phrase, challenge, or creator behavior. A trend may change the **entry format** (opening, editing rhythm, prompt, or creator collaboration), but it cannot replace the product proof, participation rule, destination, or KPI. Write `趋势状态：已通过 / 有条件通过 / 本轮无合格候选 / 未来扫描` for every coverage-matrix row.
