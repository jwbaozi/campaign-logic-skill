# Marketing Moment & Campaign Idea Engine

Use for a named marketing moment or event: holiday, seasonal moment, 618, Double 11/12, new-product launch, anniversary, sport event, exhibition, cultural moment, or hot topic. Extract **all** named moments and product-launch/important communication requirements from the brief. Read `knowledge/09-marketing-moments/node-and-launch-playbook.md` plus a relevant node reference such as `teacher-day.md` when it exists.

## Coverage is mandatory

Start with a `节点与上新覆盖矩阵`. Each input node/launch must have one row; do not treat “Teacher's Day” as the only task merely because it is the most elaborated example. Include: requirement, timing, relevance/role, target action, direct gameplay, conversion destination, KPI, trend status, and key risk.

For a detailed request, turn every row into a node/launch card. If the brief lists four items—such as new product launch, Spring Festival, Christmas, and Teacher's Day—the answer must contain direct recommendations for all four. For a product launch, use a product-proof mechanism rather than forcing a holiday-style emotional idea.

For each card, invoke `workflows/node-benchmarking.md`. Compare the named client's prior comparable work, industry peers, and named competitors only when sources permit it. If they are unknown, explicitly mark the comparison `【缺失】` and tell the user which names, materials, dates, markets, and results to obtain from the client contact.

## First make the relevance decision

Determine and state:

1. Whether the moment is relevant to the brand/product and why.
2. The user’s changing emotion, behavior, context, and purchase reason.
3. The credible role the brand can play.
4. How the product value enters naturally rather than as a forced logo placement.
5. The single user action the campaign must earn.

If the relationship is weak, say so and propose a smaller, credible participation role or a different moment. Label unknown inputs as `【需要补充】` and planning assumptions as `【示例假设】`.

## Trend rule

Do not call a remembered meme “current”. When the user asks for a current trend, route to `workflows/trend-scan.md` and scan every near-term coverage-matrix entry. Use only recently captured, attributable evidence that passes brand fit, platform-policy, copyright/personality-right, and public-sentiment checks. For a future node, state `在实际发布前 T-14 至 T-7 重新扫描并做平台、权益与品牌安全核验` instead of recommending today's meme months in advance. Give a durable non-meme execution route alongside every trend route.

## Creative territory requirements

Unless explicitly asked for a concise answer, create at least three distinct territories. Vary the underlying behavioral mechanism, not merely content names. Possible distinctions include emotional resonance, product experience, social participation, content/IP, data/tool utility, event, public-interest/culture, and community co-creation.

For each territory provide:

1. Creative Territory
2. One-sentence Big Idea
3. User participation mechanism and reward
4. Core content/formats and the product proof point
5. Social spread mechanism and content asset created
6. Roles for KOL/KOC/talent/brand account when relevant
7. Online actions and suitable offline actions
8. Commerce, store, mini-program, website, or private-domain destination
9. Media amplification: audience, placement, creative test, scaling, and retargeting
10. KPI by objective, execution difficulty, major risk, and fallback

Compare the territories against the chosen objective, brand fit, feasibility, and likely differentiation. Do not disguise a repeated “topic + KOL + livestream + giveaway” combination as different ideas.
