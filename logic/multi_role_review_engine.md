# Multi-role Review Engine

Use only after the user confirms they want multiple-role review of a substantive proposal, text feedback, or visual report. Treat every role as a decision lens, not an actual stakeholder statement or approval.

## Select lenses

Use the roles named by the user. If the user says yes but does not specify, propose these four default lenses and proceed only after they accept them: brand/business decision-maker, strategy lead, media/execution lead, and sales/e-commerce/CRM lead. Add risk/compliance only when the category, claim, data, creator, public-interest, or public-sentiment risk warrants it.

## Role card contract

For each selected lens, write:

1. **决策标准**：what this role must protect or approve.
2. **会追问什么**：the evidence, assumption, or causal link they would challenge.
3. **主要风险**：what fails if the current material remains unchanged.
4. **需要确认/批准什么**：the smallest fact, owner, resource, or decision required.
5. **建议动作**：the practical repair and who should own it.

Use only evidence in the supplied material and previously stated assumptions. Do not invent internal politics, budgets, approval processes, stakeholder preferences, or claims.

## Role-specific lenses

### 品牌/业务决策

Test business priority, brand fit, trade-off, resource logic, and whether the plan earns a decision. Distinguish brand/result aspirations from measurable commitments.

### 策略负责人

Test the evidence→audience→insight→strategy→Core Idea chain; remove framework decoration and identify the one decision that changes the strategy.

### 媒介/执行负责人

Test touchpoint roles, asset readiness, production/approval dependencies, timing, channel rules, test-and-scale logic, ownership, and contingency.

### 电商/销售/CRM负责人

Test offer, conversion destination, lead/purchase definition, inventory/service readiness, data handoff, remarketing, and retention value.

### 风险/合规负责人

Test data claims, creator/third-party rights, platform and sector policy, sensitive-topic handling, participation rules, and crisis fallback.

## Consolidation

After role cards, deduplicate issues into a single priority list: `must decide before proposal`, `must validate before launch`, and `can optimize after launch`. State unresolved disagreements as trade-offs, not false consensus.
