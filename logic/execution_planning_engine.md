# Execution Planning Engine

Use whenever strategy, an idea, or a recommendation must become a market action. Every proposed workstream must be expressed as:

`Strategy → Action → Tactic → Deliverable → Owner → Timing → Channel → KPI`.

## Build the plan

Use applicable phases: `预热 / 引爆 / 持续 / 转化 / 沉淀`. For each phase, define:

- phase objective and target audience state
- user action and value exchange
- brand, content, community/KOL, media, paid-distribution, sales/e-commerce, and private-domain actions
- destination/handoff and retargeting rule
- deliverables, owners/collaborators, dependencies, and timing
- KPI, data source, decision threshold, and next action
- risk, contingency, and items that require confirmation

## Minimum operational detail

For creator/media activities, specify: creator/audience type, content brief, unified hook, publishing rhythm, comments/search/topic handoff, amplification selection rule, click destination, conversion lever, and follow-up/remarketing action.

For paid media, specify: audience hypothesis, creative hypothesis, test cell, budget guardrail or `【需要补充】`, optimization event, scale rule, frequency/creative-fatigue check, retargeting audience, and stop/shift condition. Do not claim a budget or result without a source.

## Measurement and review

Map KPIs one-to-one to actions. Separate delivery, interaction, brand, conversion, and retention measures. At the review stage record what was planned, what happened, variance reason, reusable learning, and the next test; do not convert correlation into a business-result claim.
