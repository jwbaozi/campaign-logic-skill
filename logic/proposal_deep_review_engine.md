# Proposal Deep Review Engine

Use for an uploaded marketing proposal, campaign deck, brief, framework, or text that the user asks to inspect or improve. Work from the supplied material; do not fabricate missing pages, data, or facts.

## Review sequence

Assess only the applicable material, in this order:

1. Brief comprehension and commercial problem
2. Commercial goal, marketing goal, target behavior, and measurability
3. Market/industry evidence and the judgment it actually supports
4. Audience: priority segment, need, barrier, motivation, and situation
5. Insight: fact versus a tension that changes the strategic choice
6. Strategy and Core Idea: derivation, choice, and creative guidance
7. Creative system: unified mechanics rather than unrelated activities
8. Communication path and roles of content, channel, KOL/KOC, media, activity, and commerce
9. User path: see → engage → become interested → act → buy/leave lead → share/return
10. Operations: feasibility, sequence, assets, owners, dependencies, resources, budget assumptions, and risks
11. KPI map and the post-campaign learning plan

## Issue card

For every key issue, use this exact information order:

```
### [classification] [chain link]
- 原方案写了什么：quote or faithful summary; use `【未提供】` if absent.
- 判断：one decisive conclusion.
- 为什么：evidence and reasoning, including the broken upstream/downstream link.
- 会造成什么后果：decision, execution, measurement, or client-risk impact.
- 建议怎么改：specific structural or strategic change.
- 可直接替换/补进方案的内容示例：client-ready wording, clearly labeled `【示例假设】` where source facts are missing.
```

## Output order

1. Material boundary and overall judgment
2. `【可保留】` content and why it earns that status
3. Ranked issue cards, with the causal chain shown where needed
4. A repaired one-sentence strategy and revised outline only if supported or requested
5. Customer-facing, spoken-presentation, internal-confirmation, and internal-only items
6. Missing information, impact, and practical search/validation route

Do not turn a review into a mandatory full methodology exercise. If a framework is useful, state: why it fits the precise question, the conclusion obtained, and the next strategy decision it changes.
