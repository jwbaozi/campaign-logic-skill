# Trend Scan Workflow

Use only when a request explicitly needs a current meme, hot topic, viral sound, creator format, or platform trend. Trend data decays quickly; never rely on model memory alone.

## Capture evidence

1. Search the platform's current trend/creative center or official marketing/creator guidance first; then corroborate with at least one reputable industry source.
2. Build one trend row per near-term node/launch rather than one generic trend list. Record platform, source URL, capture date/time, trend name/format, visible evidence of momentum, audience, and likely lifecycle stage. A source older than 14 days is context, not proof of “current”.
3. Verify current platform content, advertising, copyright, personality-right, sector, and public-sentiment restrictions before recommending use.
4. For a future node or holiday, do not scan today and claim the finding will still be current. Write `在实际发布前 T-14 至 T-7 重新扫描并做平台、权益与品牌安全核验`, and give an evergreen, non-trend mechanism now.

## Score before use

Reject or downgrade a trend when it fails any non-negotiable check: brand/product fit, community sensitivity, user safety, legal/rights clearance, platform policy, or execution window. Then score 1–5 for relevance, participation fit, product integration, controllability, and likely lifespan.

## Output

Use this short evidence card before a trend recommendation:

```
【趋势快照】名称/形式：
平台与来源：
抓取时间：
为什么现在相关：
品牌适配判断：通过 / 有条件通过 / 不建议
可用方式：把趋势用于哪一个内容入口；产品价值如何进入
风险与替代：
```

Always include a non-trend creative route that achieves the same user action. Never present a discovered trend as owned IP, a guaranteed traffic source, or a substitute for a campaign mechanism.
